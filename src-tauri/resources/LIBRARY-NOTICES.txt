Folio Hebrew Library — personal offline reference collection

Texts downloaded from TorahLM / TorahApp's official repository:
https://github.com/TorahApp/TorahLM-download
Upstream source: Sefaria, https://www.sefaria.org
TorahLM licensing notice: https://torahlm.org/sefaria_license
Sefaria licensing guide:
https://help.sefaria.org/hc/en-us/articles/18490043237148-How-to-Find-and-Understand-Licensing-or-Copyright-Information

Every book's edition, license label, source URL, and source-file SHA-256 are
stored in library.sqlite. The source snapshot commit is in its metadata table.
The reading interface inserts passage text only. Edition, license and source
metadata are retained in the bundled database and this internal notice.

The corpus includes Public Domain, CC0, CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, CC-BY-NC and unspecified
licenses. In particular, the William Davidson vocalized Talmud editions are
generally marked CC-BY-NC. Keep attribution, respect noncommercial and
share-alike terms, and check individual edition rights before redistribution.
An unspecified upstream license is not a grant of redistribution rights.
The software's licensing does not relicense the source texts.

Hebrew and Aramaic source text only. English translations are excluded.
Formatting is converted from Markdown to plain text for reading and insertion;
vowels and cantillation are retained. A separate normalized search index omits
combining marks and normalizes final letter forms. Content and names follow
the upstream edition, including traditional attributions to Rashi.

Intended library use: free personal/noncommercial use, as specified by the
publisher on 26 October 2026. Each edition retains its own applicable terms;
this intent does not replace missing permissions or relicense any text.

Tanakh is displayed as 39 component books (the traditional 24-book canon,
with Samuel, Kings, Chronicles, Ezra-Nehemiah and the Twelve split for browsing).
Bavli includes 37 tractates with Gemara in this catalog. The Jerusalem Talmud
is excluded at the user's request. Shekalim, whose Gemara is from Yerushalmi,
is therefore not included in this Babylonian-only collection.
Rashi is included on all 39 Tanakh component books and the 36 Bavli tractates
for which this source supplies a Rashi text. Rashi does not cover every passage.
No Rashi on Tamid is supplied. No other author is substituted.

Mishnah includes all 63 tractates in six orders, 4,192 mishnayot, with 63
Bartenura commentary texts (21,204 comments). Browsing follows tractate,
chapter and mishnah numbering. Pirkei Avot includes the source's sixth chapter
(Kinyan Torah). Bartenura follows the source's coverage; a commentary is not
invented for a mishnah without a source comment. No Rashi is added to Mishnah.
The Mishnah texts are marked Public Domain upstream. Bartenura edition labels
are preserved individually, including noncommercial and unspecified labels.
